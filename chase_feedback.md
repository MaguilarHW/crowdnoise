- The current design seems to provide a way to view the "provenance" (initial version) of each sound clip. I think it might be interesting to hear the provenance of the song as a whole -- compile all the unedited clips and see how they sound together.
- About the collectible sample cards: how exactly do you "get" or use them? I like the idea of sharing your best clips, but what exactly makes them "collectible"? Can you take someone's clip and put it in your song, even if you're not in a group together? Or are they just things you can browse and listen to? I personally prefer the latter.
- the README mentions "difficulty rankings and rewards," allowing you to earn rewards for remaking difficult songs. What kinds of rewards could you get? Perhaps higher visibility for your remake?
- It's important to keep a minimalist, simple design. This is pretty much a full music recording + editing + extra features for collaboration + music streaming app -- it's vital that all these complex features remain accessible and easy to understand. I commend your design for upholding this rationale.
